## Your brief

The scope's survey, context, container map, technologies and open questions, and
the threat model. The context and map give the people and external systems the
system deals with and what each can reach, each container's roots, technology,
size, entry points, outputs and relationships, and the trust boundaries. The map
is a starting point, not a limit, and it may be wrong or incomplete: where the
code disagrees, trust the code.
